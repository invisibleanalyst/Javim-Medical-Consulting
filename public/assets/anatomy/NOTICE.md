# Anatomy model credits

The heart, selected blood vessels, thyroid, liver, pancreas, ribs, clavicles and sternum in `torso.glb` are adapted from:

- **Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy — CC BY-SA 4.0**. Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomy), Marcin Zielinski (Blender atlas). Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy, revision `e214005da8dc0a226176165f0a4d4e32f2155e0f`.
- **BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science — CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan**. Original models by Kousaku Okubo. Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html.

Adaptations for Javim: selected torso structures, cropping, surface smoothing, mesh reduction, browser GLB export, and new viewer materials. The adapted model is distributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Model modifications must retain attribution and the same licence. The anatomy renderings in `docs/screenshots/anatomy*.png` also depict this adapted asset; retain these credits when reusing those visuals. This asset licence does not apply to unrelated website code.

The atlas credits third-party kidney and inner-ear models with noncommercial licences. **Those meshes are not included.** The two kidney shapes are independently authored, simplified illustrative geometry, included under CC BY-SA 4.0 as part of this adapted asset. Their placement follows anatomical coordinates, but their shape is not a clinical model.

This viewer is for exploring laboratory services. It is not a diagnostic, surgical or educational anatomy reference. The source atlas also carries anatomical limitations; professional review is appropriate before using it for medical instruction.

See `torso-manifest.json` for the included structures and their origin.
